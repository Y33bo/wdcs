Events

Join us at our Christmas Party from 1pm - 3pm on Saturday 5th December 2026.

Come and join us for our annual Christmas Party - this is a highlight of our year and our biggest event!

This year we are also working alongside the Deaf Support Team to host a joint Christmas party 5th December 1-3pm at Kings Hawford School Barn, Hawford Lock Lane, Claines, Worcester, WR3 7SD

Tickets are £10 per family and include activities and a gift from Father Christmas for each child.

Games, Fun, Crafts, Father Christmas Visit & Refreshments all available.

When booking, please include all names and ages of children so that the Elves can get to work!

Worcester Deaf Children's Society have booked x50 tickets for our families to come along to the BSL performance of Aladdin at Malvern theatre.

As always, we have subsidised the tickets for our families to help to make it more affordable and accessible. We have secured seats with a super view of the stage and the BSL interpreter.
